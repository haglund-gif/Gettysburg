CH1-001-S01 | White House of Confederacy 1863
Duration: 10 | Location: Richmond, Virginia
People: Robert E. Lee, Jefferson Davis | Sources: Library of Congress
Caption: Jefferson Davis's home, the "White House of the Confederacy," next to State Capitol of Virginia

Richmond, Virginia, the spring of eighteen sixty-three. General Robert E. Lee has come to call upon President Jefferson Davis, at the stately residence that Richmonders know as the White House of the Confederacy.

==================================================
CH1-001-S02 | Lee arrives on Traveller
Duration: 10 | Location: Richmond, Virginia
People: Robert E. Lee
Caption: Robert E. Lee arrives on his grey horse Traveller at Jefferson Davis's home, the "White House of the Confederacy."

General Lee arrives upon his faithful grey horse, Traveller, and makes his way up the drive beneath the blossoming magnolias.

==================================================
CH1-001-S03 | Lee meets Davis
Duration: 10 | Location: Richmond, Virginia
People: Robert E. Lee, Jefferson Davis
Caption: Lee enters the room and meets Davis.

The General is shown into the President's study, where Mr. Davis rises to receive him. Two men, bound together by a cause, and by the weight of a long war.

==================================================
CH1-001-S04 | Davis congratulates Lee
Duration: 10 | Location: Richmond, Virginia
People: Robert E. Lee, Jefferson Davis
Caption: "Congratulations on your stunning victory at Chancellorsville."

Mr. Davis offers his warmest regard. "Congratulations," says he, "on your stunning victory at Chancellorsville."

==================================================
CH1-001-S05 | Lee mourns Jackson
Duration: 10 | Location: Richmond, Virginia
People: Robert E. Lee, Jefferson Davis
Caption: "But I lost my right arm of Stonewall Jackson. Maybe he will recover."

But the General's thoughts are elsewhere. "I have lost my right arm in Stonewall Jackson," he answers. "Maybe he will recover."

==================================================
CH1-001-S06 | Two major issues
Duration: 10 | Location: Richmond, Virginia
People: Robert E. Lee, Jefferson Davis
Caption: Davis: "Quite a loss for us for now. We have two major issues to discuss."

The President replies gravely: "Quite a loss for us, for now." He then turns to the matters at hand. "We have two major issues to discuss."
