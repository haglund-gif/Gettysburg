




## Speaker Notes Requirements

For every slide, create a metadata block at the beginning of the Speaker Notes.

Populate the values based on the content of that specific slide.

Do NOT copy sample values from other slides.

Generate appropriate values for each field based on the scene, people, location, and narration represented on that slide.

Use this exact format:

SlideID: [slide ID]
Title: [slide title without ID]
Duration: [duration in seconds]
Location: [primary location]
People: [comma separated people]
Units: [comma separated units or blank]
Sources: [historical sources if known]
Keywords: [comma separated keywords]
MediaType: [Image, Video, Map, Animation, or Mixed]
Status: Draft

Narration:

[slide narration]

For every slide, populate Location, Latitude, and Longitude whenever a specific geographic location is known.

Use decimal degrees.

Examples:

Latitude: 37.5407
Longitude: -77.4360

Latitude: 39.8309
Longitude: -77.2311

If the location is not yet known, leave the fields blank.



Example only:

SlideID: CH1-001-S03
Title: Lee Meets Davis
Duration: 120
Location: Richmond, Virginia
People: Robert E. Lee, Jefferson Davis
Units: Army of Northern Virginia
Sources: Library of Congress
Keywords: Gettysburg Campaign, Richmond, Invasion Planning
MediaType: Video
Status: Draft

Narration:

Following the victory at Chancellorsville...

Do not reuse the example values above except when they are actually correct for a slide.
